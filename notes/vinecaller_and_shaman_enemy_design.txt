VINECALLER AND SHAMAN - ENEMY DESIGN NOTES

These are normal enemies, not bosses.

The purpose of both enemies is to add tactical decisions and room mechanics rather than simply having higher stats.


==================================================
VINECALLER
==================================================

ROLE

Battlefield-control and timing enemy.

The Vinecaller should punish players who mindlessly stay in melee range and spam attacks.

Its primary mechanic is a temporary Thorn Shield.


THORN SHIELD

Duration:
Approximately 4 seconds.

Visual telegraph:
The Vinecaller creates a clearly visible circular particle effect around itself, with particles rising upward to make the shield obvious.

The shield should be visually distinct enough that players immediately understand:

"Attacking this enemy right now is dangerous."


DAMAGE BEHAVIOR WHILE SHIELDED

The Vinecaller is NOT fully invulnerable.

While the shield is active:

- The Vinecaller only receives approximately 20-25% of the original incoming damage.
- The attacker receives reflected damage equal to approximately 50% of the damage they dealt.
- Critical hits are included in the calculation.

Example:

If a player lands a critical hit dealing 100 damage:

- The Vinecaller receives approximately 20-25 damage.
- The player receives approximately 50 reflected damage.

This makes attacking the shield a deliberate risk/reward decision.

The player can:
- Stop attacking and wait.
- Attack anyway and accept the reflected damage.
- Potentially use the reduced damage window strategically.

The reflected damage should be handled consistently with the existing combat system and should respect intended defensive calculations.


KNOCKBACK

When a player attacks the Vinecaller while Thorn Shield is active:

- The player should receive strong knockback.
- The knockback should make repeatedly standing directly beside the Vinecaller difficult.
- The effect should feel like hitting a dangerous thorn barrier.

This should not be unavoidable damage without feedback. The shield must be clearly telegraphed.


SHIELD EXPIRATION - THORN BURST

When the 4-second Thorn Shield expires:

- The shield visually breaks.
- Thorn projectiles are fired outward.
- Fire approximately 4 to 6 projectiles.
- The projectiles should be distributed evenly around the Vinecaller in a circular pattern.
- Each projectile travels in a straight direction.
- Players hit by a thorn projectile take damage.

The Thorn Burst should be dodgeable.

The expiration should have a clear visual and/or audio indication so players who are paying attention can react.

After the shield expires, the Vinecaller should have a cooldown before it can activate Thorn Shield again.

This creates a combat rhythm:

1. Shield warning.
2. Dangerous 4-second shield period.
3. Thorn Burst.
4. Vulnerable period.
5. Shield becomes available again later.


VINECALLER DESIGN INTENT

The Vinecaller is not meant to be a boss or a major room-wide controller.

Its purpose is to create a timing and positioning problem inside normal encounters.

Players should learn:

- Do not blindly spam melee attacks.
- Watch enemy states.
- Decide whether reflected damage is worth continuing to attack.
- Move away when the Thorn Burst is about to occur.


==================================================
SHAMAN
==================================================

ROLE

Support, disruption, and target-priority enemy.

The Shaman should be an enemy that players actively need to consider instead of simply ignoring until every other enemy is dead.


ALLY SUPPORT

The Shaman can provide a moderate buff to nearby living enemies.

The buff should be visually communicated, such as through particles or another clear effect.

Possible support effects can include moderate improvements to enemy effectiveness, but the Shaman should not simply make nearby enemies overwhelmingly stronger.

The purpose is to create target priority:

"Do we kill the Shaman first, or do we deal with the other enemies?"


DEFENSE CURSE

The Shaman should have an offensive curse targeting players.

The curse reduces player defense by a percentage rather than subtracting a fixed amount.

Example concept:

- Player is hit by the curse.
- Their current effective defense is reduced by a percentage for a limited duration.
- The effect then expires.

Using a percentage makes the effect relevant across different stages of progression.

It can affect both weak and highly progressed players without relying on one fixed defense value.

The exact percentage and duration should be balanced later.

The curse should have a clear visual indication and should not be confusingly hidden from the player.


ON-DEATH TOTEM

When the Shaman dies, it should spawn a temporary enemy-supporting Totem somewhere appropriate in the current room.

The Totem is an entity/mechanic, not a collectible item.

When created:

- It selects approximately 2 or 3 nearby living enemies.
- Those selected enemies become invincible.
- A visible line/beam of particles connects the Totem to every protected enemy.
- Protected enemies should have an additional visual indicator if needed.

The particle connections must make it immediately obvious which enemies are currently invincible and why.


TOTEM HEALTH AND DEFENSE

The Totem should:

- Have a substantial HP pool.
- Have no special defensive mechanic.
- Not block or negate incoming attacks.
- Simply be damageable until destroyed.

This means the player cannot ignore it forever, but it also does not become mechanically frustrating to attack.


TOTEM DESTRUCTION

When the Totem is destroyed:

- All linked enemies immediately lose invincibility.
- The particle links disappear.
- Any Totem-related temporary state is cleaned up.

If a linked enemy dies through another mechanic or disappears:

- Its link should be safely removed.

The Totem should also safely clean itself up when:
- The room ends.
- The encounter ends.
- The dungeon/run ends.
- The world or room becomes unavailable.


SHAMAN DESIGN INTENT

The Shaman should create a target-priority decision.

While alive, it:
- Supports nearby enemies.
- Applies a percentage-based defense curse to players.

When killed, it:
- Creates a temporary Totem.
- The Totem protects 2-3 remaining enemies with invincibility.
- Players must decide whether to immediately destroy the Totem.

This means killing the Shaman first is useful, but can also temporarily change the encounter instead of simply removing the support enemy.


==================================================
ENEMY SYNERGY
==================================================

The Vinecaller and Shaman should work well in the same room without being designed as a mandatory pair.

Vinecaller:
Controls timing around attacking and positioning.

Shaman:
Controls target priority and temporarily supports other enemies.

Together, they can create encounters where players need to:

- Decide which enemy to attack first.
- Watch for enemy states.
- Manage reflected damage.
- Dodge Thorn Bursts.
- React to defense curses.
- Destroy Totems when enemies become invincible.

The goal is to make normal rooms more tactical without turning these enemies into bosses or relying on inflated health and damage values.
